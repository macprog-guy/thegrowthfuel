Want the secret to leading a high-performing team? You can learn it from dog
training —or here in this post.

If your team isn't engaged, I learned something surprising in dog training...

...Since not everyone has a Malinois to test this technique on, here it is:
🐕 If a dog doesn't trust your leadership, it won't listen.

The same goes for your team.

Leaders try to fix engagement with:

>> Motivation tactics - everyone sees them as fake from a mile away
>> Incentives & rewards - creating competition by pitching employees against
   each other
>> Team-building exercises - superficial, without any follow-up

But here's the truth: If your energy is off, none of it works.

(Yes, people today are more tense and reactive. How you show up as a leader
makes a bigger impact than ever.)

Instead, try focusing on these three things:

1️⃣ leadership presence
 → if you're overwhelmed, your team mirrors that energy.

2️⃣ authenticity
 → stop copying leadership formulas—own your unique strengths.

3️⃣ ability to reset quickly
 → great leaders regulate themselves before they try to regulate others.

📌 Want a more engaged team?

✔ Start by managing your energy first.
✔ Walk into your next meeting centered—not rushed.
✔ Watch how differently people respond.
