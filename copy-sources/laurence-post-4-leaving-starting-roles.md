# Laurence's post 4 — How you leave a job, and how you start the next one

Provided 2026-07-06. Parked in the library — place deliberately, per the
"about them / 90% on the reader" rule.

---

When I left a company, a manager told me:
"You know, as you leave, you don't need to do so many extras."

I smiled internally and thought: Actually, yes, I do.

Not just for the team staying behind—but for me, too.

>> Because how you leave matters.
>> And how you start your next role matters just as much.

Too often, leaders rush into their next role or next hire without fully
closing the last chapter.

And it shows:

- job seekers bring old frustrations into interviews
- hiring managers make decisions based on past baggage, not fresh perspectives
- teams feel the unresolved tension of leaders who haven't fully moved on.

Here's a short reset I'd suggest - A step most leaders skip:

1️⃣ What part of my last role am I still holding onto? (Frustration,
resentment, unfinished conversations?)

2️⃣ What will I do differently in my next role because of it?

If you don't pause to reset, you don't really move forward—you just repeat
old patterns.
