## 1. Servant leadership backfires
(Already in library as post-2.) Serve ≠ surrender · empathy without authority
= confusion · clarity builds trust · evolve without losing who you are.

## 2. Three essential elements
Self-awareness ("where you stand today is not where you stood a year ago") ·
Boundaries ("you need to create and defend them — theoretically you know;
in practice it's tricky for a seasoned leader") · Empathy with boundaries.
GOLD: "You can understand without approval."
GOLD: "You need to value your value." ← tagline-grade; candidate for the
concept-unification session.

## 3. Kind leadership means different things (podcast teaser)
Different cultures/backgrounds craft different healthy leadership. Minor —
context for the servant-leadership theme.

## 4. Throw away the cheat sheet
Books about other leaders' 5 qualities don't fit you. "What worked for them
might not work for you, and that's okay."
GOLD: "Throw away the cheat sheet on becoming a great leader and switch to
working on your core assets."

## 5. Disagreeing without a rooster fight
Express your view without dragging someone down; understand without agreeing.
GOLD: "I believe in win-win or no game."
State boundaries calmly and carry your point — or drop it.

## 6. No magic pill (habits derail)
Life derails happen; no quick fix.
GOLD (candor, very Kao): "I don't have a quick fix I can share immediately
with you. That would be false, and I won't do that to you."
GOLD: "There's always a solution — slower and realistic."

## 7. The 20-qualities slide is unrealistic
No one embodies 20 behaviours between two emergencies. Instead: build on
strengths & failures · develop your unique style · create space to stay
centred. Reinforces #4 (anti-formula theme).

## 8. The second elephant (too fast / pushy) — HER "too tough" story
"I was sometimes considered too fast or pushy… I was the second elephant in
the room. I cannot turn back time… but I learned a lot, and I'm more flexible
while keeping my focus." — first-person proof of the too-tough side (mirror
of the "trop gentille" story).

## 9. Problems don't get easier up the ladder
C-suite confidant story (holiday call before midnight).
GOLD: "Problems remain and even get more complex. But what changed is me."
GOLD: "To get a fresh perspective, you need an external perspective." ←
the why-coaching argument, in one line.

## 10. The Terminator / cat & tiger — THE too-tough-or-too-kind post
Manager fires a destructive team member, gets called "Terminator."
Too harsh → people don't dare speak up; too soft → pushback isn't heard;
either way "our leadership will be questioned in both cases."
GOLD (the metaphor): "You may think you are a pussycat, and others think you
are a scary tiger — or vice versa."
Self-awareness + feedback are the way out.
