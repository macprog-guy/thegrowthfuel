# Laurence's post 3 — Careers need foundations, like houses

Provided 2026-07-06. The actual "too tough or too kind" post is still to come —
she's looking for it.

---

Building a house teaches you a lot.
A little engineering. A little architecture. A lot of diplomacy.

So when you finally get the keys?
It's a milestone moment. You're there to stay.

But here's something curious:
→ When given the choice between investing more in foundations or finishes,
most homeowners choose… the exteriors.

That sounds wild, right?

You're probably thinking, "No way, I'd invest in the foundations."

Okay—so here's the real question:
Do you do that with your career?

Because careers, like homes, need two things to last:
1. Solid foundations — the mindset, skills, and strategy beneath the surface
2. Regular maintenance — not just reacting before things fall apart

And yet... how many people wait until burnout, restructuring, or missed
promotions to take action?

Are you building a career that looks strong or is strong?

PS:
This reflection came from two places:
An engineer who mentioned it on LinkedIn and my father and grandfather—both
architects—introduced me to the topic when I was 16.

I still remember sitting in a café in Paris, watching them scribble ideas on a
napkin with their colleagues and former teachers. The way they talked about
foundations—with passion, not just precision—stayed with me.
