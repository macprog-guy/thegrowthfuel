FAQ

Common questions

How does the one-on-one work?

We start with you — understanding your challenges, goals, and leadership style. Together, we'll co-create a personalised plan that evolves as you do. Forget one-size-fits-all solutions. This is a tailored approach designed to meet you where you are and take you where you want to go.

How long do clients typically sign up for?

A focused 2.5-hour session helps identify a specific challenge you've overlooked, enabling you to tackle it with immediate, actionable solutions. For long-term progress, a 3 to 6-month framework addresses broader leadership milestones — with a key milestone at 4 weeks to decide next steps.

What results can I expect?

Clients seamlessly transition into new leadership roles, lead through challenging transitions or difficult conversations, and navigate major life changes — such as returning from parental leave or recovering from burnout.

I don't have much time. Can it still work?

Coaching integrates into your busy schedule. With one-hour sessions every second week, you'll apply insights in real time. This keeps the process light on your calendar but powerful in impact.

What type of clients do you work with?

Senior leaders across industries, including pharmaceuticals, finance, legal, consultancy, start-ups, and Fortune 100 companies. If you're a driven leader ready to grow and thrive, we could be a great match. I always set up a free discovery call first so we can see the fit and decide — plus there are Go/No-Go milestones to give you flexibility and the guarantee that you can choose what comes next.
