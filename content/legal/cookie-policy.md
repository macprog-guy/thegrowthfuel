## Cookie Policy

**Last updated: 1 January 2025**

This Cookie Policy explains how Rethink Performance Sarl ("we", "us", "our"), the company behind The Growth Fuel, uses cookies and similar technologies on our website thegrowthfuel.com.

### What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to website owners.

### Cookies We Use

#### Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They are usually set in response to actions you take, such as setting your privacy preferences or filling in forms.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Cookie consent | Remembers your cookie preferences | 1 year |

#### Analytics Cookies

We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. These analytics do not use cookies that track you across other websites and do not collect personally identifiable information.

#### Third-Party Cookies

When you use our booking system (powered by Calendly), Calendly may set cookies on your device. These cookies are governed by Calendly's own privacy and cookie policies. We embed Calendly only on our booking page and only when you interact with the scheduling widget.

### Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

- **View cookies** stored on your device
- **Delete** individual or all cookies
- **Block** cookies from specific or all websites
- **Set preferences** to be notified when a cookie is set

Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

**Browser-specific instructions:**
- [Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT201265)
- [Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)
