## The Coach Behind the Fuel

I'm Laurence Methot, and I've spent more than two decades helping leaders find clarity at the crossroads of their careers. Whether you're navigating a corporate transformation, stepping into a bigger role, or quietly questioning what comes next, I've been in that conversation thousands of times — across boardrooms, virtual calls, and quiet cafes in cities on every continent.

My career began in organisational psychology, where I studied how people thrive (and stall) inside large systems. That academic foundation — a PhD focused on leadership development and organisational change — gave me a deep appreciation for the invisible structures that shape careers: culture, politics, identity, and timing.

## My Approach

I don't believe in one-size-fits-all frameworks or motivational platitudes. My coaching is bespoke, evidence-based, and grounded in genuine relationship. Here is what that looks like in practice:

**Rigorous Listening.** Before I offer a single question, I listen. Deeply. The real challenge is rarely the one you present first. My job is to hear what's underneath — the pattern, the assumption, the unspoken ambition.

**Strategic Clarity.** Together, we move from swirling complexity to a clear, actionable path. I draw on psychometric tools, stakeholder analysis, and systems thinking to help you see the full picture and decide where to place your energy.

**Courageous Accountability.** Growth requires honesty. I will challenge you — respectfully, directly — when I see you playing smaller than you need to. And I'll celebrate the wins with you, because those matter too.

## Philosophy

I believe that the leaders who change organisations for the better are the ones willing to change themselves first. Coaching is not remediation; it is an investment in your next level of impact.

The best leaders I've worked with — across the public and private sectors, from Geneva to Singapore to Washington — share one quality: the willingness to stay curious about themselves, even when it's uncomfortable.

> That's the fuel I help you find. Not motivation that fades by Friday, but the deep, renewable kind — the kind that comes from alignment between who you are and the work you choose to do.

## A Global Perspective

Having lived and worked across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific, I bring a genuinely cross-cultural lens to every engagement. I understand that leadership looks different in different contexts, and I help you leverage your unique cultural intelligence as a strength.

I'm based in Switzerland and work with clients worldwide — in person and virtually.
