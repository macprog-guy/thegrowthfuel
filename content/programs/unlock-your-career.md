## The Unlock Your Career Programme

You're good at what you do — and you know it. But somewhere between your current role and the leadership position you want, there's a gap. It might be visibility. It might be executive presence. It might be a story you keep telling yourself about what's possible.

Unlock Your Career is a structured coaching programme for ambitious mid-career professionals who are ready to move from functional expert to strategic leader.

### What's Included

The programme runs over **10 weeks** and is designed to produce tangible career acceleration:

- **Five 75-minute coaching sessions**, held every two weeks
- **A career positioning assessment** — where you stand, where you want to go, and what's in the way
- **Executive presence and personal brand workshop** (virtual, 2 hours)
- **A stakeholder mapping exercise** to identify sponsors, allies, and decision-makers
- **A written career strategy document** you can use for years to come
- **Email support** between sessions

### Who It's For

This programme is ideal for professionals with 8-18 years of experience who:

- Are targeting a director-level or VP-level role within the next 12-24 months
- Feel overlooked despite strong performance
- Want to shift from being valued for execution to being valued for strategic thinking
- Are navigating a career transition — across functions, industries, or geographies
- Work in international or cross-cultural environments and want to leverage that experience

### How It Works

**Phase 1 — Diagnose (Weeks 1-3).** We assess your current career positioning with rigour. Where do others see you? Where do you want to be seen? What's the gap — and is it real or perceived?

**Phase 2 — Position (Weeks 4-7).** We build your leadership narrative, sharpen your executive presence, and map the relationships and moves that will get you noticed by the right people.

**Phase 3 — Move (Weeks 8-10).** You execute. Whether it's a conversation with your manager, an application for a new role, or a strategic visibility play, you leave this programme with momentum and a plan.
