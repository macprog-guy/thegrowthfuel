## The Growth Fuel Membership

Transformation doesn't happen in a single programme. The leaders who sustain their growth over years — not just quarters — are the ones who invest in ongoing reflection, accountability, and community.

The Growth Fuel Membership is designed for leaders who have completed a coaching engagement (with me or another qualified coach) and want to maintain their edge. It combines regular individual coaching with peer learning in a curated group of senior professionals.

### What's Included

Your membership includes:

- **One 60-minute individual coaching session per month** with Laurence
- **Access to a monthly peer learning circle** (90 minutes, virtual) with 6-8 fellow senior leaders
- **Quarterly leadership theme workshops** on topics like influence, resilience, strategic thinking, and career architecture
- **Priority scheduling** for additional coaching sessions at a member rate
- **A private community forum** for asynchronous discussion, resource sharing, and peer support

### Who It's For

The membership is designed for:

- Leaders who have completed a coaching programme and want continued support
- Senior professionals who value a peer network of thoughtful, ambitious leaders
- Executives who want regular, structured reflection time built into their calendar
- Anyone who has worked with me before and wants to stay connected to the work

### The Peer Learning Circle

One of the most powerful elements of the membership is the monthly peer circle. These are not networking events. They are structured, facilitated conversations where senior leaders share real challenges, offer honest feedback, and learn from each other's experience.

Members consistently report that the peer circle is where some of their biggest breakthroughs happen — not because I'm coaching them, but because they're coaching each other.

### Investment

The Growth Fuel Membership is **CHF 450 per month** with a minimum commitment of three months. After that, you can continue month to month or cancel anytime.

Current coaching clients receive their first month free when transitioning to membership.
