## The Reignite Programme

You've had a successful career by any measure. The titles, the responsibilities, the track record — it's all there. But something has dimmed. Maybe it was a restructuring that left you sideways. Maybe it's the creeping feeling that you're running someone else's playbook. Maybe you simply woke up one morning and realised you were going through the motions.

Reignite is a focused, high-intensity coaching programme designed for experienced leaders who are ready to stop coasting and start choosing again.

### What's Included

The programme runs over **12 weeks** and combines individual coaching with structured reflection exercises:

- **Six 90-minute coaching sessions**, held every two weeks
- **A comprehensive leadership and values assessment** at the start
- **A personalised "Career Architecture" workbook** to map your next chapter
- **Two laser-focused 30-minute calls** between sessions for real-time challenges
- **A capstone session** to crystallise your forward plan and accountability structure

### Who It's For

Reignite is designed for leaders with 15+ years of experience who:

- Have achieved conventional success but feel disengaged or restless
- Are considering a major career move but can't see the path clearly
- Have been through a setback — a layoff, a passed-over promotion, an organisational change — and need to rebuild with intention
- Want to lead with more authenticity and less autopilot

### How It Works

**Phase 1 — Excavate (Weeks 1-4).** We go beneath the surface. Through assessment, deep conversation, and targeted exercises, we identify what's really driving your dissatisfaction — and what still lights you up.

**Phase 2 — Architect (Weeks 5-8).** With clarity on your values, strengths, and non-negotiables, we design the next chapter. This isn't blue-sky dreaming; it's practical, strategic planning grounded in who you actually are.

**Phase 3 — Ignite (Weeks 9-12).** You take action. We work through the inevitable resistance, refine the plan, and build the habits and structures that will sustain your momentum long after our work together ends.
