Despite the employment stability, the public sector is not immune to common issues such as burnout and team and intergenerational conflicts.

Addressing the team's journey is vital to having a thriving public service that serves the community at its best.

Seeking external coaching shows courage and accountability to face challenging times with the best tools available.

Team Building
Team building implies three key steps:

1- Initial team and leader interview to create a bespoke event to identify and address the key challenges and goals.
2- Group collaboration, allowing offline discussions to ensure that every member is at ease and can express his needs and thoughts to benefit the group dynamic. 
3- Follow up so the team and the organisation can benefit from the initial effort.

Disruption or Conflict In A Team
Nobody is at ease with disruption or conflict, but we all must face it at one point.
Whether someone had to be fired, the team lost a key element, or a restructuring created a void, it is key to recreate a new group dynamic which includes the previous events so that the new foundation created is solid and authentic.


Burnout Support
Burnout affects competent and driven leaders. There's a lot to unpack:
- various feelingss including surprise, self-doubt, denial or shame from the person who fell sick;
- tiredness, frustration or resentment due to the additional workload on the remaining team that needs to double down its efforts to compensate.
The only healthy way to re-create a team, is to ensure that both parties get the best support and strategies to face the next steps in their journey. 


A Leaders' First 100 Days
When you step into a new executive role, or execute on a growth or innovation plan, you set the direction. Your presence and approach has an impact on many.
