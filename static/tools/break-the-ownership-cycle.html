A self-audit

The Blame & Ownership Cycle

Changing roles is a challenge. Here’s a 5-minute assessment to help you take charge of your future and find your next opportunity. And it starts with one small step you can take this week.

Nothing here is submitted anywhere — it saves in your own browser.

Saves automatically as you type
Step 1

What’s standing between you and the leader you want to become

Four lines, fast, without editing yourself. Write what you’d say to a friend, not what you’d say to a recruiter.

Step 2

What you could do about each one

Take your four lines in order. For each, write one move you could make without anyone’s permission. Not a solution — a move. “Update my CV” and “refresh my LinkedIn” doesn’t count: you have done those, and they changed nothing. If a line has no move, leave it blank and notice which one it was.

Stuck on one? Ask yourself: what would make them see you differently? Where does your natural style get misread — and what would you adjust, without becoming someone else? Who already sees you the way the decision-makers need to, and what could they do?

Step 3 · the only line that matters

One move this week

Pick it from step 2. In my experience the move is rarely tactical — it is something in you: where you stay too soft, too tough, or simply keep waiting. Small and specific beats ambitious and vague.
